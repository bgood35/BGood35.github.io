<!-- ===== BOURSIN ===== -->
<article id="boursin">
<p class="eyebrow">Case Study · Groupe Bel (Boursin) · 2025</p>
<h2>Accelerated Boursin's household-penetration growth with a social-first approach to seasonal campaigns</h2>
<div class="metrics">
<div class="metric"><div class="num head">+1.1 pts</div><div class="lab">Household-penetration gains vs. prior year (52 weeks)</div></div>
<div class="metric"><div class="num head">+28%</div><div class="lab">Sales volume vs. prior year (52 weeks)</div></div>
<div class="metric"><div class="num head">2x</div><div class="lab">Consideration &amp; purchase-intent lift, A25–34 vs. prior year</div></div>
</div>
<div class="label">The Challenge</div>
<p>Boursin's annual holiday activation, "Maison Boursin," was becoming less effective at recruiting new seasonal buyers, and the custom content required to execute the campaign limited the brand's financial flexibility.</p>
<div class="label">The Approach</div>
<p>Research showed Boursin's growth target looked nothing like its current brand or heavy-category buyers — justifying a shift from pulling buyers in with content to pushing content out on social. Boursin showed younger consumers how it elevates social gatherings, with fun, aspirational (but achievable) charcuterie-board ideas on TikTok and Meta.</p>
<div class="label">The Result</div>
<p>The shift accelerated household-penetration gains among the core audience — specifically Gen Z, who had been underserved by Boursin's prior content- and PR-led holiday activations.</p>
</article>

<!-- ===== GOGO SQUEEZ ===== -->
<article id="gogo">
<p class="eyebrow">Case Study · Groupe Bel (GoGo squeeZ) · 2024–2025</p>
<h2>Applied an audience-led approach to return GoGo squeeZ to household-penetration growth</h2>
<div class="metrics">
<div class="metric"><div class="num head">10 mo.</div><div class="lab">Sales decline reversed</div></div>
<div class="metric"><div class="num head">+10 pts</div><div class="lab">New-to-brand share of sales (2025 vs. 2024)</div></div>
<div class="metric"><div class="num head">+0.9 pts</div><div class="lab">Household-penetration growth over two years</div></div>
</div>
<div class="label">The Challenge</div>
<p>Despite strong ROAS in Shopper Marketing channels, GoGo squeeZ was losing household penetration as younger consumers aged out of the brand and rising competition created headwinds for recruiting new buyers.</p>
<div class="label">The Approach</div>
<p>Shifted focus from lower-funnel metrics (ROAS) to unique reach, leveraging custom content on social, YouTube, and Minecraft to drive brand reappraisal among lapsed and non-brand buyers. Engineered an integrated media strategy and measurement across consumer and shopper tactics, putting more support behind differentiated products and new innovations to better justify the brand's premium price.</p>
<div class="label">The Result</div>
<p>GoGo squeeZ's household penetration continues to grow, outpacing the category — especially among households with kids aged 12–17, which grew +1.7 pts over the past year.</p>
</article>

<article id="goldfish">
<p class="eyebrow">Case Study · Campbell (Goldfish) · 2023–2024</p>
<h2>Recaptured Gen Z attention during cultural moments to grow Goldfish's household penetration</h2>
<div class="metrics">
<div class="metric"><div class="num head">5.5x</div><div class="lab">Benchmark household-penetration lift (households without kids)</div></div>
<div class="metric"><div class="num head">4</div><div class="lab">Clio Awards</div></div>

<div class="label">The Challenge</div>
<p>Goldfish's perception as "a snack for young kids" capped its growth — the brand struggled to resonate with Gen Z and couldn't risk alienating parents.</p>
<div class="label">The Approach</div>
<p>Goldfish leveraged its brand equity to spark Gen Z participation on social, using younger-skewing tentpoles. It surpassed earned-media benchmarks by using the live GRAMMYs broadcast to invite people to share their own jingles on TikTok and Twitter. The brand then used social to tease — and the 2023 NBA All-Star broadcast to announce — a limited-edition Boban Marjanović "Hand Dish."</p>

<!-- ===== HSBC ===== -->
<article id="hsbc">
<p class="eyebrow">Case Study · HSBC (Checking Accounts) · 2019</p>
<h2>Transformed HSBC into a full-funnel marketer to improve media effectiveness and efficiency</h2>
<div class="metrics">
<div class="metric"><div class="num head">−48%</div><div class="lab">Customer acquisition cost vs. prior period</div></div>
<div class="metric"><div class="num head">+400%</div><div class="lab">Long-term ROAS based on customer lifetime value</div></div>
<div class="metric"><div class="num head">Above bmk.</div><div class="lab">Improved application submission rates</div></div>
</div>
<div class="label">The Challenge</div>
<p>As a challenger in the US market, HSBC leaned heavily on aggressive rates, offers, affiliates, and conquesting larger banks in search. That approach limited both the volume and quality of new applicants, resulting in higher-than-category-average CAC.</p>
<div class="label">The Approach</div>
<p>Built brand consideration among HENRYs (High Earners, Not Rich Yet) by shifting dollars from incentive-based campaigns — which drove volume of low-quality applicants — into custom content with leading publishers. Simplified consumer banking into a single campaign across tiers (Choice, Advanced, Premier) to maximize reach, sharpened targeting with Dynamic Creative Optimization, and built one landing page that let visitors choose the offer right for them.</p>
<div class="label">The Result</div>
<p>The campaign brought in more — and higher-value — customers. Upper-funnel costs were quickly offset as more people arrived via brand terms rather than conquesting larger banks. Application submission rates rose despite heavier traffic, because visitors were routed to a page that let them pick the offer they were most qualified for.</p>
</article>
